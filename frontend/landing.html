The CSAS signature algorithm

The CAMP Signature Alignment Score (CSAS) blends TF-IDF semantic similarity with a curated lexical-affinity score, then shapes the result with a Bloom's-taxonomy cognitive gate before quantizing to a 0–3 strength level.

// Compute alignment strength

strength(CO, PO) = quantize(

a, b: configurable signal weights (sum to 1)

bloom_gate: cognitive gate in [0,1] from the CO's Bloom level
